The forecast said three degrees. The car said one. By seven the lettuce had a white crust on it and the last courgettes had given up.

What came in the night before: two crates of squash, the green tomatoes for chutney, and every chili still on the plant.

^^^Pick green tomatoes before the first frost. They ripen on a windowsill in a week or two.^^^
